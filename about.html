Our Story

Born in Toronto, Committed to Clean Living

Toronto EcoClean Solutions started in 2018 when our founders realized how many traditional Toronto cleaning companies relied heavily on toxic bleach, harsh synthetic fragrances, and ammonia products that triggered allergies in pets and children.

We set out to revolutionize urban home cleaning across the Greater Toronto Area. By combining plant-based eco-friendly formulas with high-performance HEPA vacuum technology and rigorously trained cleaning professionals, we created a service that leaves homes spotless and chemically safe.
